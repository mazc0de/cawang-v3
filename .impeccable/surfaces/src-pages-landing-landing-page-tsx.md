# Surface brief: Cawang landing page (/)

Scope: the public landing page, rebuilt from nothing (the previous page is discarded at the user's request). Mode: Persuade. World: Tanggal Gajian (DESIGN.md), logo colours and Manrope binding.

Audience/job: young Indonesians deciding whether to sign up. They must believe all three at once: budgets run from their own payday; Safe-Pay shows what is truly spendable; logging takes two taps. Action: "Daftar gratis" (signed in: "Buka Dasbor"). Proof: the product's own components with labelled sample money. No testimonials, counts or prices exist; none are invented. The Wikimedia photographs are dropped.

User feedback on the old page: too empty, generic, unconvincing.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The logo is the hero: its branch rises through the first viewport and its three coins are the three balances (Total, Terkunci, Tersedia), live and controllable. Refuses the centred-headline-over-cards page and the split hero with a phone.

OWN-WORLD: Leaf ground #F6FAEE; branch strokes #2F6B2F drawn thick and round like the mark; coins in logo gold #E9B21A with the white inner ring; green shell #1F4D27 for the closing band and primary buttons; Manrope 800 headlines (max 6rem), figures tabular with the small quiet "Rp". Hairline-bordered white panels for the product proofs, no shadows at rest except the coins' soft drop.

STORY: In the first viewport the visitor reads what Cawang tells them (what is left until payday), sees the three coins, flips the Safe-Pay switch and taps a quick spend to watch Tersedia change; below, the Cycle strip proves payday-based months, a two-tap logging panel proves speed, a plain list names everything else, and a green band closes with the sign-up.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (logo, Fitur, language, theme, Masuk, Daftar gratis). Left column: h1, one-paragraph lead, Daftar gratis + Masuk, then "Coba catat" quick-spend chips. Right: the branch from the bottom edge, top coin largest = Saldo Tersedia with the countdown, left coin = Total saldo, right coin = Dana terkunci with a Safe-Pay switch. Sample-figures note under the hero. Phones stack copy, then the branch at full width.

FORM: dealt surface structure "Cabang tumbuh", re-roll round 1, dealt position 3 of 3 (option 2); seed:ae32d62325c0ab13.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction and motion

Toggling Safe-Pay empties or refills the gold coin and Tersedia recomputes; quick spends lower Total and Tersedia. One orchestrated load moment: the branch draws upward, then the three coins settle in. Reduced motion: everything static.
