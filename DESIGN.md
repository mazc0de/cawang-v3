# Design System: Cawang

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Tanggal Gajian"**

Cawang is a clean, familiar finance app whose whole identity is its logo: a deep branch-green stem that splits into three gold coins. The system takes the mark's three materials literally. The branch green becomes the chrome (sidebar, mobile top band, the Dashboard balance band, the landing's closing band and the primary buttons), the pale cream behind the favicon becomes the page ground, and the coin gold is held back for the mark itself and for money that is locked aside. Everything the user reads sits on flat white cards with hairline green-grey borders, set in Manrope with tabular figures.

The rhythm of the product is the payday, not the calendar, and the one signature component makes that visible: the Cycle strip, every day from this payday to the next in a single row, payday ringed, today filled green, a quiet red bar for each day's spending and a gold dot where a Safe-Pay falls due. It appears on the Dashboard and, unlinked, as proof on the landing page. The landing goes one step further and draws the logo at page scale, so its three coins hold the three balances (Total, Dana terkunci, Saldo Tersedia) and move when the visitor flips Safe-Pay or taps a quick spend.

Density is calm but working: cards carry real rows, figures are large and bold, labels are small and muted. Motion is nearly absent. The app has one authored moment (today's cell settling in the Cycle strip) and the landing has one orchestrated moment (the branch draws, the coins settle). Light and dark are both first-class; dark turns the ground near-black green and the shell a deep forest band. It refuses the grey-card-plus-one-accent default, themed costume, and the centred-headline-over-feature-cards landing.

**Key Characteristics:**
- The logo's own three colours are the palette: branch green chrome, coin gold accent, leaf cream ground.
- Semantic money colours are fixed and never borrowed: green Income, red Expense, slate blue Transfer, amber/gold Locked Funds.
- Flat white cards with 1px hairline borders and 20px corners, never nested.
- Manrope everywhere; money in tabular figures with a smaller, quieter "Rp".
- The Cycle strip is the signature; payday sets the rhythm of every summary.
- One authored motion per surface family, all of it static under reduced motion.

## Colors

A three-material palette lifted straight from the logo (branch green, coin gold, leaf cream) plus four binding semantic money colours, each with a pale "soft" partner for tinted backgrounds.

### Primary
- **Branch Shell Green** (#1f4d27): the chrome. Desktop sidebar, mobile top band, Dashboard balance band, auth-page side panel, landing closing band, and the fill of primary buttons, the centre add button and today's cell in the Cycle strip. In dark mode the shell deepens to Forest Night (#11291a) while primary buttons lift to a brighter Leaf Button Green (#2f7a37) so they stay visible on the near-black ground.
- **Logo Stem Green** (#2f6b2f): the stroke colour of the mark, the focus ring, the brand accent (feature icons, the payday ring, `brand` text links via Brand Ink #1f4d27) and the first chart series. Inside the shell the stem re-points to Pale Stem (#a7d69a) so the mark stays legible on green; in dark mode the stem is #6fb35f and the brand accent is #8fcb7e.

### Secondary
- **Coin Gold** (#e9b21a): the coins of the logo, nothing else by default. It fills the three coins in BrandMark and favicon.svg (with a 55% white inner ring) and the gold dot on a Cycle strip day where a Safe-Pay falls due. Inside the green shell the Locked token re-points to Coin Gold, so the Dashboard's Dana Terkunci figure reads gold on green.
- **Coin Ink** (#4d3800): the dark brown-gold text on a filled coin, used only on the landing's hero coins.

### Tertiary: semantic money colours
- **Income Green** (#23803a, soft #dcf2df): money in. Deliberately fresher and lighter than the shell so income never reads as chrome.
- **Expense Brick** (#c2412b, soft #fbe6e1): money out, over-budget status and destructive actions. The Cycle strip's spending bars use it at 60% opacity.
- **Transfer Slate** (#3f6698, soft #e2eaf5): money moving between Accounts.
- **Locked Amber** (#8a5a00, soft #fcf0cf): Locked Funds and Safe-Pay on light grounds (text, pills, the hatched pending segment of the Budget plan bar). Dark mode uses #f0be45 on #2b2310.
- **Caution Orange** (#b4520f, soft #fdebd9): a Budget running low; never money itself.
- **Category palette** (`src/lib/palette.ts`): ten hues (blue, sky, teal, green, lime, orange, rose, pink, brown, slate), each a pale tile with its own dark ink and a dark-mode pair, plus the mid-tones in the frontmatter for chart marks. There is no amber, violet or indigo category.

### Neutral
- **Leaf Ground** (#f6faee): the page background, the same cream as the favicon's square. Dark: #0c120d.
- **Card White** (#ffffff): every card, popover, input and chip. Dark: #141c15 (popovers #19221a).
- **Raised Leaf** (#edf3e3): tinted full-width bands such as the landing's logging section. Dark: #18211a.
- **Leaf Wash** (#ebf1e1): secondary buttons, muted fills, hover rows, the Budget track. Dark: #1d271e.
- **Field Ink** (#15211a): body text and headings. Dark: #e9f0e3.
- **Moss Muted Ink** (#556657): labels, descriptions, the "Rp" mark's base colour. Dark: #a2b29e.
- **Hairline** (#dfe7d3): every card and divider border. Dark: #243025.
- **Input Stroke** (#c9d5bb): input borders, unchecked switch track, scrollbar thumb. Dark: #34433a.
- **Shell inks** (Shell Ink #f1f6ea, Shell Muted #b9cdb1, Shell Raised #2a5e32): text, secondary text and raised tiles inside the green shell.

### Named Rules
**The Binding Money Colours Rule.** Green is Income, red is Expense, blue/neutral is Transfer, amber/gold is Locked Funds and Safe-Pay. These four never trade places, never decorate, and the brand never borrows them: the shell green is deliberately darker than Income green so the two cannot be confused.

**The Shell Is Chrome Rule.** Branch Shell Green fills only chrome: the desktop sidebar, the mobile top band, the Dashboard balance band, the auth page's side panel, the landing's closing band, and primary buttons (including the centre add button and today's Cycle cell). Content cards are never green. Inside a `.shell` surface the shared tokens re-point to shell inks; a page card placed inside one must restore the page tokens with `.unshell`.

**The Coin Gold Rule.** Gold appears on the logo and on Locked Funds / Safe-Pay, nowhere else. The one named exception is the landing hero, which draws the logo itself at page scale: its three coins (Total, Terkunci, Tersedia) are gold because they are the mark, not because that money is locked. Nowhere else may gold carry money that is not locked.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Manrope Variable (with Manrope, Inter, system-ui)
**Body Font:** Manrope Variable (same stack)
**Label/Mono Font:** none; figures use Manrope with `tabular-nums`

**Character:** One geometric-humanist sans in every role, made expressive by weight alone: extra-bold (800) with tight negative tracking for headlines and balances, regular for reading. Money is always tabular and always pairs large bold digits with a small, quiet "Rp".

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, clamp(36px, 5.6vw, 4.5rem), 1.02, -0.035em): the landing hero h1 only, balanced wrapping. It tops out at 4.5rem.
- **Headline** (800, clamp(30px, 3.6vw, 3rem), 1.06, -0.03em): landing section headings and the closing band title.
- **Headline List** (800, clamp(26px, 2.8vw, 2.25rem), -0.025em): the landing's plain feature-list heading.
- **Balance** (800, 38px/44px on phones, 44px/52px from lg, -0.025em, tabular): Saldo Tersedia in the shell band. The auth sample card uses 32px/40px; the landing logging demo uses 36px.
- **Page Title** (800, 24px on phones, 28px/36px from lg, tight tracking): each app screen's h1.
- **Title** (700, 16px): card headings ("Cycle ini", "Hari ini", "Budget").
- **Figure** (700, 17px, tabular): income/expense/net figures inside cards; shell tiles use 16px.
- **Body Lead** (400, 17-18px, 1.625, max 48-52ch): landing leads under headlines.
- **Body** (400, 14-15px, 1.43): rows, descriptions, card text.
- **Label** (400-500, 12-13px, muted): figure labels, sidebar group labels, sample-figures notes. Never uppercase, never letter-spaced.
- **Micro** (600, 10-11px): weekday letters in the Cycle strip, bottom-nav labels.
- **Currency Mark** (500, 0.72em of its figure, 70% opacity, 0.2em right margin): the "Rp" before every amount, including masked amounts.

### Named Rules
**The Quiet Rp Rule.** Every IDR amount renders through the Money component: tabular figures, "Rp" at 0.72em, weight 500, 70% opacity. Never set "Rp" at the digits' size or weight.

**The 6rem Ceiling Rule.** Display type never exceeds 6rem; the shipped landing h1 stops at 4.5rem.

## Layout

The app is a two-zone shell. From lg (1024px) a fixed 240px green sidebar sits at the left and the content column runs to a 1200px max width with 40px side padding; below lg the sidebar is replaced by a 56px green top band and a fixed 64px white bottom nav with a raised centre add button, and the content pads 16px (24px from sm). The Dashboard's balance band bleeds edge to edge under the mobile top band (rounded 28px only at its bottom corners) and becomes a normal 20px-corner card on desktop. Cards stack with 16px gaps on phones and 24px from lg; the Dashboard below the Cycle card splits 3fr / 2fr on desktop.

Inside cards the gutter is 16px on phones and 20px from sm; card headers sit 16px from the top, rows are 12px tall-padded with hairline dividers between groups. Phones never let two large figures collide: income and expense share a row, net takes its own.

The landing runs at a 1280px max width with 16px/32px gutters. The hero is a two-column grid on desktop (copy 5fr, branch 6fr, the branch allowed to bleed past the container to the right edge) and stacks copy, actions, branch, then quick-spend chips on phones. Sections are separated by full-width hairline rules and breathe 64px vertically on phones, 96px on desktop; the logging section sits on a Raised Leaf band. Auth screens split 50/50 on desktop: a 380px form column on the leaf ground and a shell panel holding a sample balance card; phones show only the form.

The Cycle strip scrolls horizontally below xl (1280px) with 20px faded edges and snaps to day cells; from xl every day shares the row evenly.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by default. Depth comes from tone, not shadow: white cards on a leaf ground, a hairline border around each card, Raised Leaf bands and Shell Raised tiles for a second tier. Shadows exist only where something genuinely floats above the page.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Add Button Lift** (`box-shadow: 0 6px 16px -4px rgb(31 77 39 / 0.45)` plus a 4px ring in the card colour): the mobile centre add button, which rises out of the bottom nav.
- **Coin Drop** (`box-shadow: 0 18px 40px -18px rgb(120 90 0 / 0.6)`): the filled gold coins of the landing hero, a warm soft drop under each.
- **Sample Card Float** (`box-shadow: 0 24px 60px -24px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.45)`): the sample balance card floating on the auth page's shell panel.
- **Overlay** (kit `shadow-md` / `shadow-lg` with a 1px foreground/10 ring): popovers, menus, selects, sheets and dialogs over a 40% black scrim.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Card Rule.** Cards at rest have a 1px hairline border and no shadow. Only the four floating roles above carry a shadow.

## Shapes

Generous, friendly rounding that echoes the round coins and the round caps of the branch strokes. The base radius is 10px (buttons, inputs, sidebar items, category tiles); small controls step down to 6-8px. Cards and the desktop shell band use 20px; the mobile balance band's bottom edge uses 28px. Cycle strip cells are 14px, shell tiles and the centre add button 18px. Chips, pills, switches, avatar dots, the payday ring and every coin are fully round. Borders are always 1px hairlines; the only dashed strokes are meaningful (the next-payday ring at the end of the Cycle strip, and an emptied coin when Safe-Pay is switched off on the landing). The Budget plan bar draws pending Safe-Pays as a 45° amber hatch after the spent fill.

## Components

### Buttons
Confident and plain: solid shell-green fill, white text, no gradients.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (10px); small sizes 8px.
- **Primary:** Branch Shell Green fill, white text, 40px tall, 16px side padding, 14px weight 500. Landing calls to action use the large size at 48px tall, 24px padding, 16px text.
- **Hover / Focus:** hover fades the fill to 80%; focus shows the ring colour at 50% as a 3px ring plus a ring-coloured border; pressing nudges the button down 1px; disabled drops to 50% opacity.
- **Outline:** page-ground fill with a hairline border, hover to Leaf Wash. **Secondary:** Leaf Wash fill (the payday stepper's minus/plus). **Ghost:** transparent until hover. **Destructive:** Expense at 10% as fill with Expense text, never a solid red slab.
- **On shell:** inside the green closing band the primary becomes a white button with shell-green text, and the secondary is a transparent button with a 40% white border.

### Chips
- **Amount presets:** white, hairline border, fully round, 36px tall, 14px weight 500 tabular "+5K"; each tap adds its value; a muted reset chip appears once an amount exists. Press scales to 95%.
- **Quick-spend chips (landing):** the same shape at 36-40px, a label plus a signed Expense amount.
- **Status pills:** 24px fully round, soft fill with matching ink: Caution for running low, Expense for over, Leaf Wash for safe. The Locked Funds pill uses Locked Amber on its soft partner with a lock icon.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 20px.
- **Background:** Card White (dark #141c15).
- **Shadow Strategy:** none at rest (see The Flat Card Rule).
- **Border:** 1px Hairline.
- **Internal Padding:** 16px on phones, 20px from sm; card footers and figure rows are divided by a hairline top border.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** Card White fill, 1px Input Stroke border, 10px radius, 40px tall, 12px side padding; 16px text on phones (no zoom), 14px from md. Dark mode fills with Input Stroke at 30%.
- **Focus:** border turns to the ring colour with a 3px ring at 50%.
- **Error / Disabled:** Expense border with a 20% Expense ring; disabled at 50% opacity on an Input Stroke wash.

### Navigation
- **Sidebar (lg+):** 240px Branch Shell Green column; Wordmark at top; muted 12px group labels; 36px items with 16px icons at stroke 1.75, Shell Muted text; hover fills Shell Raised at 60%; the active item fills Shell Raised with white semibold text. Footer holds install, settings, an avatar dot in Pale Stem with shell-green initial, and the version.
- **Mobile top band:** 56px shell band with the Wordmark and header utilities (amount toggle, notifications, language, theme).
- **Bottom nav:** fixed white bar, 64px, five cells with 20px icons and 11px labels; active is Brand Ink and bold; the centre is a 52px Branch Shell Green add button with 18px corners lifted 20px above the bar. "More" opens a bottom sheet with 20px top corners.

### Shell Balance Band
The Dashboard's first block: Saldo Tersedia at Balance size with an amount-visibility toggle, then two Shell Raised tiles (18px corners) for Total Saldo and Dana Terkunci; the Locked figure reads in Coin Gold because the shell re-points Locked to gold. On phones the band continues the top bar edge to edge; on desktop it is a 20px card with the tiles at the right.

### Cycle Strip (signature)
Every day of the current Cycle in one row, closed by the next payday. Each cell is 40px wide (equal shares from xl), 14px corners: a 10px weekday, a 28px round date (24px from xl), a 6px gold dot slot for a Safe-Pay due date, and an 18px slot for a spending bar (Expense at 60%, square-root scaled, 3px rounded top). Payday is ringed 2px in brand green; today is filled Branch Shell Green with white text and its bar in white at 60%; future days are muted; the next payday is a dashed brand ring labelled "Gajian". Days up to today link to that date's Transactions; on public pages the strip is unlinked. On load the row scrolls so today sits centred on a cell edge, and today's cell plays the app's one authored motion, `today-settle` (scale 0.86 to 1 with a fading primary halo, 700ms, ease-out-expo `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, 150ms delay).

### Landing Logo Hero (signature)
The BrandMark redrawn at page scale: round-capped Logo Stem strokes (trunk 7, branches 6 on a 100-unit grid) rising from the hero's bottom rule, carrying three Coin Gold discs with the mark's 55% white inner ring. The largest top coin is Saldo Tersedia with the countdown to payday; the left coin is Total saldo; the right coin is Dana terkunci with a Safe-Pay switch (shell-green track when on). Switching Safe-Pay off empties that coin to a dashed gold outline, fades its branch to 30% and recomputes Tersedia; quick-spend chips lower Total and Tersedia. Coin text scales with container units. Load moment: each branch draws upward (`branch-draw`, 900ms, ease-out-expo, staggered 0/250/350ms), then the coins settle from 82% scale (`coin-settle`, 700ms, at 600/700/850ms). Coins are visible from the first frame; under reduced motion everything is static.

### Money
Every amount renders through one component: tabular figures, the Quiet Rp, a true minus sign (−) for expense and negative values, "+" for signed income, tone classes for the four money colours plus muted for zero. Hidden amounts keep the "Rp" and show a letter-spaced mask.

### Budget Plan Bar
A 10px fully round Leaf Wash track; the spent part fills brand green (safe), Caution (running low) or Expense (over); pending Safe-Pays follow as an amber 45° hatch, never counted as spent.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep the four money colours binding: Income green (#23803a), Expense red (#c2412b), Transfer blue (#3f6698), Locked amber (#8a5a00 on light, gold inside the shell).
- **Do** put Branch Shell Green (#1f4d27) only on chrome: sidebar, mobile top band, Dashboard balance band, auth side panel, landing closing band and primary buttons.
- **Do** wrap any page card that sits inside a shell surface in `.unshell` so its tokens return to the page set.
- **Do** render every amount with the Money component (tabular, Rp at 0.72em, 70% opacity).
- **Do** use flat white cards with a 1px hairline border and 20px corners, separated by 16px (phones) or 24px (desktop) gaps.
- **Do** design every screen in light and dark together; both are first-class.
- **Do** label any sample money on public pages as made up.
- **Do** honour reduced motion: `today-settle`, `branch-draw` and `coin-settle` only run under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use gold for money that is not locked. The landing hero's three logo coins are the single named exception, because they are the mark.
- **Don't** fill content cards, list rows or charts with shell green; it is chrome.
- **Don't** nest a card inside a card; divide a card's interior with hairline rules instead.
- **Don't** add shadows to cards at rest; only the add button, the hero coins, the auth sample card and overlays float.
- **Don't** add a second authored motion to the app or section entrance animations to the landing; the app has `today-settle`, the landing has its one branch-and-coins moment.
- **Don't** set display type above 6rem.
- **Don't** use amber, violet or indigo as a category colour.
- **Don't** set "Rp" at full size and weight beside the digits.
